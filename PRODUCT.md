## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, deployed as a static site to GitHub Pages.

## Users

- **Primary: HTL Leonding students.** They want to find out what the club is, decide whether it's for them, see when the next session is, and join. Students matter most, and every decision should serve them first.
- **Secondary: external guests.** People from outside the school who may attend events as guests. They need to know what a session is about, when and where it happens, and that guests are welcome.

## Product Purpose

The website of **"Algorithmic Problem Solving with AI"** (short name: AI Club), a student club at HTL Leonding (https://www.htl-leonding.at/). The club meets regularly to learn about AI, practice using it, and discuss current developments.

The site has two jobs:

1. **Discover & join:** explain what the club is and get students to sign up.
2. **Events & sessions:** show upcoming and past sessions so members and guests know what is happening and when.

Success means students who land on the site understand the club quickly and join through the sign-up form, and members and guests can always find the next session.

## Positioning

A hands-on student club built around *algorithmic problem solving* with AI. It's about practising with AI and discussing it, not taking a course about it. It is a club inside HTL Leonding, run regularly for its students and open to guests at events.

## Operating Context

- People: **Rainer Stropek** is the responsible teacher; **Benjamin Hartmann** is the club president (a student). The club is student-led with teacher responsibility, and both may be named on the site.
- Session content: learning about AI coding tools, hands-on practice, discussions of current developments, and members exchanging experiences, tricks, tips, preferences, settings, and agent skills.
- Session format: every session picks one topic (e.g. context management, tool calling, sandboxing), introduces it for beginners as a recap for semi-pros, dives into specific details for pros, and then tries it hands-on in an example.
- Audience and attendance: open to all students and to external guests. Students who attend at least half of the sessions get their participation noted on their school report. Most meetings are hybrid, so members can also join from home.
- Prerequisites: no own coding agent is required; students can use **Novedu**, the school's free coding agent.
- Calendar: sessions every two to four weeks, usually Wednesdays 17:30–19:30 (6 in winter 26/27, 8 in summer 27). The first, on Fri 23 Oct 2026 10:00–12:00, is the CCC (Cloudflight Coding Contest) at JKU Linz; all other sessions are at HTL Leonding. The club currently announces only dates, times and places: no session topics, no rooms.
- Joining happens through external **Microsoft Forms**; the site links out and does not handle sign-ups itself. Students: https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/MEzuhSPX4C (also as a QR code). External guests: https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/Dy4hPbj15k.
- Sessions are recurring club meetings: learning, hands-on practice, and discussion of current AI developments.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: no accounts, no backend, no member-only features in scope.
- Features in scope: club introduction, join call-to-action (Microsoft Form link), event/session listing.
- Out of scope for now: hosted learning content (slides, recordings), member tools.

## Brand Commitments

- Full name: **Algorithmic Problem Solving with AI**. Short name: **AI Club**.
- The club has **its own identity**. It references HTL Leonding as its home but does not adopt the school's visual branding.
- Language: **English** only.

## Product Principles

1. **Students first.** Write for an HTL Leonding student deciding whether to join. Guests are welcome, but they are the secondary reader.
2. **Next session, always visible.** The most useful fact on the site is what happens next and when.
3. **One clear way in.** Joining means one obvious path to the Microsoft Form.
4. **Practice over hype.** Present the club as people solving real problems with AI, not as AI marketing.
5. **Truthful by default.** Only show real sessions, people, and facts. Placeholders stay visibly placeholders.
